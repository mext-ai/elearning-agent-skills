# Sales and product training

Needs the MEXTY MCP. For training customers or partners outside the organisation, the LMS seats or a public post on Mextribe.

## Inputs

The product sheet, the price list, the three customers it is for, the competitors it meets, and the questions customers actually ask. If support tickets or call notes exist, they are the best source of real objections.

Mark anything not yet public. Training for partners must not contain it.

## What to build

| Need | Build |
|---|---|
| Know the product | A short course: who it is for, the problem it solves, how it works, what it costs |
| Say it well | An activity where the learner picks or orders the points of a pitch for a given customer |
| Handle objections | Scenarios: the customer says X, choose the answer; feedback explains why |
| Practise a conversation | A role-play activity with a customer persona, if the workspace has an AI activity for it |
| Prove it | A short evaluation before the launch date |

One product, one path: course, practice, evaluation. For a range, one path per product line with a shared opening course.

## Writing rules

- Facts and prices come from the sheet, word for word. Never round a price or invent a feature.
- Lead with the customer's problem, then the product.
- Each objection gets the honest answer, including "this product is not for you" when that is true.
- Claims about competitors must be ones the company can stand behind. If unsure, leave them out and ask.

## When the product changes

Put the path under `keep-training-current`: a new product sheet produces drafts of the affected slides and questions, for the product owner to review. Tell the teams what changed in one paragraph.

## Beyond the team

- **Partners and resellers**: assign the path to a partner group, without internal-only slides.
- **Customers**: a how-to course posted on Mextribe (`mextribe-community`).
- **Selling the training itself**: a landing page and an offer (`keep-training-current`, section 5).
