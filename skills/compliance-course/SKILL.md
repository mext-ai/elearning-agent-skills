# One compliance course

Needs the MEXTY MCP. Certificates and tracking need the LMS. For keeping the course in step with the policy afterwards, see `keep-training-current`.

## Before writing

- **The source.** The organisation's own policy or procedure, with its version and date. If they have none, stop and say so: a course cannot stand in for a policy.
- **Who it applies to**, and what differs by role. A warehouse and an office do not need the same safety course.
- **What a person must do differently** after it. List three to six behaviours. Everything in the course serves one of them.
- **What counts as passed**, and whether a certificate is issued.

## Structure

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Why it matters here | One real situation from this organisation's work, not a statute |
| The rules | Each behaviour: the rule in the policy's words, then what it looks like on a normal day |
| Decide | Scenarios where the learner chooses what to do. This is the course; the rest supports it |
| Where to go | Who to ask, where to report, where the policy lives |
| Check | An evaluation on applying the rules, not on recalling the policy's wording |

Keep it under 20 minutes. Annual refreshers should be shorter and built on what changed and what people got wrong last year.

## Writing rules

- Quote the policy for obligations and name the clause. Paraphrase only the explanations.
- Scenarios come from the organisation's reality: their tools, their customers, their sites.
- Wrong answers in scenarios are tempting and common, and the feedback says why, kindly.
- No legal advice. Where the policy and the law might differ, flag it for the policy owner.
- Plain language, in the language staff work in.

## The check

Build it with `assessment-design`. Each question tests a behaviour. Set the pass mark with the policy owner. Do not ask trick questions; the aim is that people pass because they know what to do.

## Record

- In the course description: the policy, its version, the owner, the date written.
- Draft, review by the policy owner, then publish (`build-together`).
- A certificate, if issued, belongs to this version of the course.

Say what the record is: evidence that named people completed this version on a date. It is not a statement that the organisation is compliant.
