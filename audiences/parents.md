# For parents

**A safe place to play, and a way to see what they learned.**

EdVerse is a store of learning games for ages 4 to 18. Your child plays; you choose what they see, how long they play, and you can ask your AI what they learned. Playing, and making games from homework, is free.

## Safe by the store's rules

Every game is tested against the same requirements before it is listed, by its author and then by a MEXTY reviewer:

- no ads, no chat, no links out of the game, no purchase buttons and no sign-up wall;
- no request for a child's name, email, photo or location, and no trackers;
- nothing violent, scary or mocking, and nothing that pressures a child to keep playing or to pay;
- wrong answers handled kindly, and sound that can be muted;
- a one-tap Report button, answered by MEXTY staff.

## Set up

1. Open [EdVerse](https://future.mexty.ai/edverse), create your account, and add a kid profile.
2. To use your AI: connect the EdVerse server at [mexty.ai/connect](https://mexty.ai/connect) and add the skills ([install](../README.md#install)).
