# For companies

**Train people, prove it, and keep it current.**

MEXTY is an authoring tool and an LMS. Your own AI assistant builds inside your workspace, from your documents: onboarding, compliance, product and AI literacy training, with the record of who completed what.

## Compliance, without the yearly scramble

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| A policy, a law or a product sheet changes | The agent reads the new version, finds the courses that cite it and drafts the changes, nothing more |
| Before learners see anything | The course owner reviews the draft, sees who is enrolled and certified, and publishes or schedules it |
| After a material change | You see who holds a certificate for the old version and reassign only what changed |
| Every week | A report: what changed, which drafts are waiting, whose certificate lapses soon |

Nothing is published, certified or put on sale without a person's yes. The record shows that named people completed a named version on a date; it is not, by itself, a statement that the organisation is compliant.

## What the workspace gives you

- **Courses, interactive activities, evaluations and learning paths**, built from your documents.
- **Drafts and review**: named reviewers, comments, what changed and who it affects, version history.
- **Roles, groups and seats**: creators and learners, with permissions per role.
- **Certificates** tied to a course version.
- **Analytics and your own dashboards**, shared with a role, a team or one person.
- **Agents and scheduled tasks** with your knowledge bases.
- **Export** to HTML and SCORM, and import of Articulate Storyline projects.

## For small and mid-size companies

You do not need a training department. One person with the company's documents and an AI assistant can build the first onboarding path in an afternoon, have a manager review it, and assign it the same day. Start with the three courses every new hire needs.
