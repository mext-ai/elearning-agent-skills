# For game developers

**Build in the browser, pass the review, get paid.**

EdVerse is a store of learning games for ages 4 to 18, with a live 3D editor and an MCP server your AI can build through. Building is free: the tools spend no MEXTY credits, so your own AI subscription is the only cost.

## How you earn

| Option | Who plays | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Anyone | An audience |
| Fixed price | Whoever buys it | 80% of each sale after the card fee |
| Pay what you want | Whoever pays your minimum or more | 80% of each sale after the card fee |
| ★ Included | Children with a learner seat | A share of the learner pool, by minutes played. MEXTY staff approve the request |

★ Included can be added on top of a price. A Mextribe community with a paid plan is a second income from the same audience ([`mextribe-community`](../skills/mextribe-community/SKILL.md)).

## The review

Every game is tested against 11 requirements, by you in the Studio and then by a MEXTY reviewer: it loads, works on every device, can be played to the end, is clear to a child of the listed ages, really teaches what it is listed for, is correct, right for the ages, stays inside the game, keeps sound under control, has an honest listing, and uses only what you have rights to. Your AI cannot take the test for you; it can make sure you pass it.

## What the platform gives you

- **A live 3D editor** shared with collaborators and their assistants. Edits merge; nothing is locked.
- **Scenes** for levels, rooms and menus, each with its own history and drafts.
- **Templates** to start from, and an asset catalogue shared by creators.
- **`@mexty/sdk`**: cloud saves private to each child, family, class and global leaderboards, achievements, the player's language, pause and mute from the store.
- **Real-time multiplayer** through the platform's relay.
- **A Suggestion box** on every game: players propose, you accept, it becomes your public roadmap.
- **Stats**: players per day, how many finish, the questions where they get stuck.
