# For universities and schools

**A curriculum as learning paths, with evidence of what students can do.**

MEXTY turns a syllabus into courses, interactive activities and evaluations, laid out as a learning path. Deliver it in the MEXTY LMS, or export to SCORM for the LMS you already have.

## How a path is built

- **One course per outcome**, in the curriculum's own wording, so every lesson and question points back to it.
- **Activities where students do the thing**: classify, order, calculate, simulate, explain.
- **One evaluation per unit and one at the end**, with points that add up and a pass mark set by the teacher.
- **Locks for prerequisites, deadlines for term dates.** Elective content sits in a parallel branch.
- **Review before release**: a colleague or the department reviews a draft; students only see what is published.

Completion, score and time for each course, and a progress line for each student. Certificates belong to a version of a course. Analytics support teaching and follow-up; they are not, alone, a basis for a decision about a student.

## Care with young learners

For under 13s: no accounts beyond what the school provides, no personal data in activities, and no open chat with an AI system inside an activity. Games from EdVerse follow the store's rules: no ads, no chat, no links out.

## Plans
